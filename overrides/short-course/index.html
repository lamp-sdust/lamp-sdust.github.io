SDUST International Programs

PROGRAM INTRODUCTION

The SDUST International Programs at Shandong University of Science and Technology bring together the University's academic resources to provide flexible learning opportunities for international and domestic students. The programs follow international academic standards and offer short-term and online courses across a broad range of disciplines.

Through rigorous and practice-based learning, students can broaden their perspectives, deepen their knowledge, and strengthen their analytical and practical skills. Students who successfully complete their courses may receive academic recognition in accordance with the requirements of each program.

STUDY PERIODS

Courses may be offered throughout the year during the following study periods:

  • Spring Courses: March–May
  • Summer Courses: June–August
  • Fall Courses: September–November
  • Winter Courses: December–February

Specific course dates, formats, and availability may vary depending on the individual program.

MODE OF INSTRUCTION

Face-to-Face, Hybrid, or Online

COURSE STRUCTURE

The International Programs cover a broad range of academic disciplines.

  • Computer Science and Digital Technologies
  • Engineering and Technology
  • Science and Mathematics
  • Earth, Energy and Environment
  • Marine and Applied Science
  • Business, Economics and Management
  • Humanities, Languages and Arts
  • Interdisciplinary and Emerging Studies

ENROLLMENT REQUIREMENTS

  • English Language Requirements

    Applicants should demonstrate an appropriate level of English proficiency for participation in English-taught courses.

  • Academic Requirements

    Applicants should demonstrate an appropriate academic background through academic records or other relevant supporting documents.
