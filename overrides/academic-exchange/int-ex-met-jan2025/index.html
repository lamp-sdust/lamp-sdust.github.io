计算机学院国际部国际交流会

计算机学院国际部邀请大家参加2025年首场国际交流会！本次交流会将介绍学校为本科生和研究生提供的暑期出国留学和访学资助机会，为同学们拓宽国际视野、提升学术能力提供资源。

活动要点

  • 了解暑期出国留学与访学的申请流程和资助政策
  • 听取往届学生的访学经验分享
  • 与国际部老师面对面交流答疑
  • 免费提供披萨、茶水和咖啡

活动时间：星期三1月8日下午 6:15–7:15

活动地点：J13-114

期待您的参与，开启国际学习之旅！

School of Computer Science International Exchange Meeting

The International Office of the School of Computer Science invites you to join the first International Exchange Meeting of 2025. This event introduces opportunities for undergraduate and graduate students to apply for summer overseas study and research funding.

Highlights of the Event

  • Learn about application processes and funding policies.
  • Hear previous students share their overseas study experiences.
  • Join a face-to-face Q&A with International Office staff.
  • Enjoy free pizza, tea and coffee.
