Program overview

Program Introduction

The SDUST International Programs at Shandong University of Science and Technology bring together the University's academic resources to provide flexible learning opportunities for international and domestic students. The programs follow international academic standards and offer short-term and online courses across a broad range of disciplines.

Through rigorous and practice-based learning, students can broaden their perspectives, deepen their knowledge, and strengthen their analytical and practical skills. Students who successfully complete their courses may receive academic recognition in accordance with the requirements of each program.
